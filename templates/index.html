    Teen safe steps

    1. Ruko. Urgency ya dhamki mein turant click ya payment mat karo.
    2. Alag se verify karo. Official app khud kholo ya known number par call karo.

    Paise chale gaye? Turant 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) par call karo ya cybercrime.gov.in par report karo. Jitni jaldi report, utni zyada recovery ki umeed.
